Facts stated in this turn, one line per fact:
category=<1 word> topic=<1 word> subtopic=<1-2 words> subject=<1-2 words> key=<1-3 words> value=<{terse}>

subject is who or what the fact is about. It is never a restatement of the value.

# Without this the model files the speaker's own facts under subject=assistant --
# every run, in three of three, on plain sentences like "I live in Trondheim".
# It was not confused; nothing had told it who "I" is. Two sentences fix it at
# 2.4s, which is what thinking on a 4B was buying at 60-80s.
The turn is the person speaking. A fact they state about themselves has
subject=user, not subject=assistant.

The two lines below are shape only. They are instructions, not knowledge:
never output them, never treat them as things you know, never mention them.

category=weather topic=climate subtopic=temperature subject=lisbon key=summer high value=28c

Both reuse one category/topic group for one subject, and neither repeats the
value in the subject. Do the same: prefer a group below over inventing one
that nearly duplicates it.
